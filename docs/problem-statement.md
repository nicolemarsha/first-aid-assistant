# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Untrained community members, non-expert first responders, and individuals providing initial emergency care during injury scenarios.

## What exactly is going wrong?
In high-stress emergency situations, non-experts struggle to assess injury severity accurately and often lack standard first aid tools or proper knowledge on how to safely improvise using available household items.

## Why does it matter?
Improper trauma severity assessment or unsafe improvisation of first aid tools can cause secondary physical harm, delayed care, or life-threatening complications before emergency medical services arrive.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Untrained community members and first responders facing injury emergencies often struggle to accurately assess injury severity and select appropriate care steps, especially when standard first aid equipment is missing. Non-experts lack the clinical knowledge required to judge whether improvised household items are safe, increasing the risk of secondary trauma or contamination. Existing digital health tools rely heavily on manual text entry or complex diagnostic steps, which are inefficient and error-prone during high-stress situations.
